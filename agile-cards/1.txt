Daily Standup
------------------------------------
Team understands the principle that we need to stand to make it quick - once finished we get back to work



Daily standup on time, fully attended and effective - 15 min only in
front of the board (physical or electronic)



Team believes in daily planning, offering help & raising impediments during standup



Parking lot concept used for detailed discussions



Whoever speaks during standup looks at everyone (not only SM/one
person) to share his/her updates
