MVP & MMP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

Ranked backlog is used to create the slices of MVPs/MMPs



Team & Product Owner collaborate to understand & decide MVP based on key personas



Prototypes are planned & executed in the least expensive manner to
validate MVP hypothesis



MVP assumptions are revisited every sprint/release 



Feedback from actual Siemens customers on the current MVP used to
re-plan future MVP's
